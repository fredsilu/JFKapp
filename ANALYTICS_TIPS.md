# 🎓 JFKApp Analytics - Tips & Best Practices

## 👥 For Restaurant Managers

### Daily Routine
1. **Check Dashboard KPIs** (5 minutes)
   - Review today's revenue
   - Check active orders count
   - Monitor delivery rate

2. **Review Top Performers** (10 minutes)
   - Which dishes sold most?
   - Who are your best clients?
   - Any anomalies?

3. **Monitor Ingredient Usage** (5 minutes)
   - What's running low?
   - Plan restocking
   - Check ingredient costs

### Weekly Review
- Compare this week vs last week revenue
- Identify best and worst days
- Plan promotions for slow days
- Review staff performance metrics (when available)

### Monthly Strategy
- Analyze monthly trends
- Plan menu adjustments
- Set revenue targets
- Budget for upcoming month

## 💡 Strategic Insights

**When Revenue is Low:**
- Check the "Top Dishes" - promote underperformers

**When Revenue is High:**
- Maintain quality standards
- Consider raising prices for top dishes

- Set minimum stock levels
- Plan delivery schedules
- Monitor ingredient costs

## 🔍 Analyzing the Charts

### Revenue Trend Chart
**What to Look For:**
- Upward trend = Good! (Business growing)
- Downward trend = Investigate (Marketing needed?)
- Spikes = What caused them? (Special event? Promotion?)
- Consistency = Stable business

**Action Items:**
- If inconsistent: Plan stabilization strategies
- If growing: Scale up operations
- If declining: Review menu, pricing, marketing

### Top Dishes Chart
**What to Look For:**
- Any surprise bestsellers?
- Are most dishes selling?
- Are expensive dishes profitable?

**Action Items:**
- Feature top dishes in marketing
- Bundle top dishes with others
- Consider discontinuing bottom dishes
- Train staff on top dish preparation

### Order Status Distribution
**What to Look For:**
- High "En préparation" = Possible bottleneck?
- Low "Livré" = Delivery issues?
- Many "En cours" = Current workload?

**Action Items:**
- If stuck in prep: Add staff or improve processes
- If delivery issues: Review logistics
- Balanced = Good operations

## 📊 Understanding the Numbers

### Average Order Value (AOV)
```
What: Total Revenue ÷ Number of Orders
Why: Indicates how much each customer spends on average

High AOV = Customers buying more/premium items
Low AOV = Customers buying basics/need upselling

Improvement:
- Upsell premium dishes
- Create combo deals
- Bundle items together
```

### Delivery Completion Rate
```
What: (Delivered Orders ÷ Total Orders) × 100
Why: Indicates service reliability

100% = Perfect delivery record
< 90% = Investigate delivery issues
90-99% = Good performance

Improvement:
- Better route planning
- More delivery staff
- Quality control checks
```

### Revenue Trends
```
Positive Trend = Revenue growing compared to previous period
Negative Trend = Revenue declining compared to previous period

Monitor:
- Day-to-day changes
- Weekly patterns
- Monthly growth
- Seasonal effects
```

## 🚨 Alert Conditions

### When to Take Action

**Revenue Down 20%+ from Previous Period**
- Check for special events that caused spike before
- Review menu prices
- Check for new competitors
- Increase marketing

**Delivery Rate Below 90%**
- Review delivery process
- Add more delivery staff
- Check vehicle availability
- Training for drivers

**No Orders in 2+ Hours**
- Technical issue or slow period?
- Check if system is working
- Review current promotions
- Check competitor activity

**New Dish Not Selling**
- Review pricing
- Train staff on menu
- Feature in marketing
- Get customer feedback

## 🆘 Common Questions

**Q: Why did revenue drop on this day?**
A: Check if it was a holiday, bad weather, or special event. Compare same day previous weeks.

**Q: Which dish should I remove from menu?**
A: Remove dishes in the bottom 20% by revenue. But verify with staff first - maybe needs marketing.

**Q: How do I increase average order value?**
A: Bundle items, suggest complementary dishes, create combo deals, raise prices on popular items.

**Q: My delivery rate is 100% but clients complain about delays?**
A: Delivery rate tracks completion, not speed. Add "delivery time" metric (future feature).

**Q: Can I export this data?**
A: Export feature coming soon. For now, take screenshots or notes.

**Q: How is revenue calculated?**
A: Revenue = (Ingredient Price × Ingredient Qty) per dish × Dish Qty + Additional Ingredients. No markup.
